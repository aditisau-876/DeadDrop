01 / PROTOCOL

Recovery authorization is separated from cryptographic possession.

DeadDrop combines governance quorum, cryptographic quorum, and blockchain-verifiable policy enforcement while keeping plaintext secrets and reconstruction off-chain.

01Encrypted SecretA trusted client encrypts the critical payload before recovery policy is applied.
02Secret SharingThe secret is split into trustee-specific cryptographic shares.
03Policy VerificationTrustee approvals are checked against the registered recovery policy.
04Client ReconstructionValid released shares are reconstructed and decrypted locally.

02 / SECURITY

Multiple controls. One verifiable recovery path.

The protocol is designed so that authorization, share validity, and recovery history can be checked independently instead of relying on one privileged backend.

Dual Quorum

Governance approval and cryptographic share requirements work together.

Share Commitments

Committed share values provide a consistency check before reconstruction.

Refresh Epochs

Share refresh cycles can preserve the secret while changing the distributed shares.

Client-Side Recovery

Plaintext reconstruction stays on the recovery client rather than the backend.

Failure Domains

Trustee placement can account for correlated operational failure.

Verifiable History

Policy decisions and recovery evidence are anchored to an auditable ledger.
